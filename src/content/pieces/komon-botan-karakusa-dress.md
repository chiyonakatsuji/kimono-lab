    Botan Karakusa (Peony and Arabesque Vine Pattern)

    The design features the Peony, known as the “King of Flowers,” symbolizing prosperity, wealth, happiness, and elegance. It is combined with the Arabesque Vine (Karakusa) pattern, whose continuously growing vines represent longevity, enduring prosperity, vitality, and the continuation of family generations. Together, these traditional Japanese motifs express a heartfelt wish for a prosperous, beautiful, and fulfilling life that continues for generations.
