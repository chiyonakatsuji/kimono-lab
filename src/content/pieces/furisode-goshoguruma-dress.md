  story: |
    Imperial Carriage and Flowing Water Pattern

    This kimono features the Imperial Carriage (Gosho-guruma) motif together with the Flowing Water (Ryūsui) pattern. The Imperial Carriage, once used by Japanese court nobles during the Heian period, symbolizes nobility, elegance, prosperity, and good fortune. The flowing water represents purity, vitality, the washing away of misfortune, and continuous growth. Complementary motifs such as plum blossoms, symbolizing perseverance and hope, and pine, representing longevity and prosperity, further enhance the design. Together, these traditional motifs express a heartfelt wish for a graceful, prosperous life filled with lasting happiness, good fortune, and continuous renewal.
